Child Safety Standards - Phasto Nasheed

Phasto Nasheed is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users. We have zero tolerance for any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), child abuse, child endangerment, grooming, trafficking, or any content or behavior that harms children.

Our Standards

Users of Phasto Nasheed must not:

  • Create, upload, share, distribute, or promote Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Engage in child exploitation, abuse, trafficking, or grooming.
  • Encourage, facilitate, or promote sexual activity involving minors.
  • Use the platform for any activity that endangers children.
  • Share content depicting abuse, exploitation, or inappropriate interactions involving minors.

Reporting Violations

Content or behavior that violates these standards may be removed promptly. Accounts involved in child exploitation, abuse, or CSAE activities may be suspended or permanently terminated. Suspected illegal activity may be reported to the appropriate law enforcement authorities where required by applicable law.

Safety Measures

  • We provide a contact channel for reporting child safety concerns.
  • We review reports related to child safety.
  • We investigate suspected violations of our child safety standards.
  • We take appropriate action against accounts that violate these standards.
  • We cooperate with relevant authorities when required by law.
